    Delete your account

    This deletes, straight away and for good:

    • your account: ID, email address, display name, created and last-used dates
    • your linked sign-in methods (Google, Apple, email)
    • your sign-in sessions on every device, so they are all signed out
    • your logged "Any questions?" questions with their answers and the readings sent with them
    • your balance, its history of credits and spends, and your badges. Any balance left is lost.
    • what you shared in "Any questions?" that wasn't approved yet or was turned down, and your app feedback
    • your roadmap votes
    • any personal daily question limit set for your account

    Car data on your phones is not touched: it was never on the server. Notes an admin already approved from what you shared stay, with nothing linking them to you. Short-lived counters expire on their own within about a day. If you sign in again later, a new, empty account is made. More about deleting
