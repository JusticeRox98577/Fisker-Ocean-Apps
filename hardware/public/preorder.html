Pre-order terms

First-revision hardware

These are the first production units. They're tested in my own Ocean before they ship, but something could still go wrong. I'll fix what I can with over-the-air updates, and I'll tell you plainly what's known to work and what isn't. If a first-revision unit turns out to have a hardware fault, I'll repair or replace it, or refund you.

Nothing is charged until there are 10

A reservation is free and commits you to nothing. When a product reaches 10 confirmed reservations, everyone who reserved it gets an email asking for the deposit. If you don't pay it, your reservation lapses and the next person moves up.

Deposits are refundable

You can cancel at any time before your unit ships, from the link in your emails, and get your whole deposit back. If I can't deliver for any reason, everyone gets a full refund.

Shipping

To the United States for now. Units ship only once they meet the FCC rules for devices with radios (they use certified LTE and Wi-Fi modules). I'll email you a realistic date when your batch is ordered.

Installing

The Nova Light plugs into the spider port behind the dash trim and takes power from a fuse tap. The SuperNova also goes inline with the center screen's video cable. There's a guide, and unplugging Nova returns the car to stock. Like any aftermarket part, it could matter to a dealer for warranty questions on related parts.

What it does to your car

By default Nova only listens to the car's networks and never sends anything to them. Remote commands on the SuperNova stay switched off until they've been tested, and you turn them on yourself.
