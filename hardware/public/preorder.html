          How it works

          1. Reserve for free. Sign the first-generation disclosure, then confirm from the email. No card, no payment.
          2. The more people reserve, the lower the price. Each product's price steps down as its batch grows, for everyone in the batch, including people who reserved earlier. You never pay more than the price when you reserved.
          3. At 10 reservations for a product, I email everyone a link to pay a refundable $100 deposit.
          4. Right before I order the parts and start building, I email everyone for the rest of the price, at the batch's final (lowest) price. That closes the batch; later reservations start the next one.
          5. Until you've paid in full, you can cancel and get your deposit back.
