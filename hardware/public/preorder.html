          How it works

          1. Reserve for free. Sign the first-generation disclosure, then confirm from the email. No card, no payment.
          2. The more people reserve, the lower the price. Each product's price steps down as its batch grows, for everyone in the batch, including people who reserved earlier. You never pay more than the price when you reserved.
          3. At 10 reservations for a product, I email everyone a link to pay a refundable $100 deposit.
          4. Right before I order the parts and start building, I email everyone for the rest of the price, at the batch's final (lowest) price. That closes the batch; later reservations start the next one.
          5. Until you've paid in full, you can cancel and get your deposit back.

          First-generation disclosure

          Please read all of this. Nova is first-generation hardware from a small company, and you should know exactly what that means before you reserve.

          1. What you're reserving

          • Nova is made by Ocean Live LLC, a small independent company. It is not made, approved or supported by Fisker or any car maker.
          • Your unit comes from the first production batch (revision 1) of a new design, built in small batches.

          2. What "first generation" means

          • It's new. Each design is tested in my own Ocean before it ships, but it hasn't had years of use in many cars. Expect bugs.
          • Not everything is finished at launch. Some features arrive later through over-the-air updates (for example remote lock and climate on the SuperNova). A listed feature may change, arrive late, or be dropped if it turns out to be unsafe or unreliable.
          • Hardware can have faults. A unit may need to be sent back for repair or replacement.
          • Later revisions will be better. A free upgrade to a later revision isn't promised.

          3. Your car

          • Installing means removing interior trim, plugging into the car's internal networks at the spider port, and taking power from a fuse tap (the screen products also go inline with the center screen's video cable). Done carelessly, it can damage trim, clips or wiring. You're responsible for installing it, or for having it installed.
          • By default Nova only listens to the car's networks and never sends anything to them. Remote commands stay off until they've been tested, and you turn them on yourself.
          • A faulty unit could drain the 12V battery or cause warning messages. Unplugging Nova returns the car to stock.
          • Like any aftermarket part, it could come up if you make a warranty claim on related parts.
          • Apps on the center screen are for use while parked, or in ways that don't distract you while driving. Don't watch video while driving.

          4. Connectivity and data

          • The Nova Light (and the Light built into the SuperNova) includes 10 years of LTE-M data. Streaming on the SuperNova or Quasar needs your own data SIM and plan.
          • Features that use the internet depend on cellular coverage and on the Ocean Live servers. How your data is handled is in the privacy policy.

          5. Price and payments

          • Reserving is free. Nothing is charged until a product reaches 10 reservations.
          • The more people reserve, the lower the price. A batch's price steps down as it grows, for everyone in it. You pay your batch's lowest price when it closes, and never more than the price when you reserved.
          • At 10 reservations, you'll be asked for a $100 deposit, refundable until you've paid in full.
          • Right before I order the parts for your batch, you'll be asked for the rest of the price. That closes the batch at its final price.
          • Once you've paid in full and the parts are ordered, a cancellation is refunded less the cost of the parts already bought for your unit.
          • If I can't deliver your unit for any reason, you get a full refund.

          6. Warranty and liability

          • If your unit has a hardware fault within 90 days of delivery, I'll repair it, replace it, or refund you.
          • Otherwise it's provided as is. To the extent the law allows, Ocean Live LLC's total liability is limited to what you paid for the unit, and it isn't liable for indirect or consequential damage. Nothing here limits rights you have that can't be limited by law.

          7. Shipping

          • United States only for now.
          • Units ship only once they meet the FCC rules for devices with radios. Dates are estimates; you'll get an email with a realistic date when your batch's parts are ordered.
