    Questions

    What does a beta tester do?

    Fit an early Nova unit in your Ocean, use it day to day, and tell us what works and what doesn't. We'll pick testers from the sign-ups in small batches.

    Does signing up mean I'll get a unit?

    No. There are only 4 test boards. If you're picked, you'll get an email with a link to say yes or no, and a no passes the board to the next person.

    Who can see my answers?

    Only the person building Nova. Your answers are private and never shared. The numbers on this page are counts only: how many people signed up and how many are interested in each product, never who.

    What happens to my answers?

    They're kept so we can plan Nova and contact you about the beta, and nothing else. Signing up again with the same email replaces your earlier answer. To be removed, write to support@oceanlive.org.
