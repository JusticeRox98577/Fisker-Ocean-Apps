Glossary

What the readings mean

Every reading here is explained with the owner guide's own words, and where to find it in the app.

  1. Home
  2. Help
  3. Glossary
Battery health
Measured capacity, usable now and rated usable. In the app: Battery→Battery health
Usable battery capacity
The kWh the app uses for range and charge time. In the app: Settings→Car
Charge and range
The charge ring on the Live tab. In the app: Live
Cell spread
Highest minus lowest cell voltage. In the app: Battery→Cells
Insulation (isolation) resistance
How well the high-voltage system is isolated from the body. In the app: Battery→High voltage
Pack temperatures
Battery and coolant temperatures. In the app: Battery→Temperatures
Power and current
Which way energy flows. In the app: Live
12V resting voltage
The 12V battery's voltage after the car has slept. In the app: Battery→See the 12V history
12V history and overnight drop
The 12V screen. In the app: Battery→See the 12V history
Parked drain
Charge lost per day while parked. In the app: Battery→Parked drain
Charging curve
Charging power against charge level. In the app: Trips→a charge
Fault code states
Active now, pending, stored, warning lamp. In the app: Faults→Scan all modules
This charge and This drive
The live session cards. In the app: Live

Quoted from the Ocean owner guide that "Any questions?" answers from. Not a dealer diagnosis.
