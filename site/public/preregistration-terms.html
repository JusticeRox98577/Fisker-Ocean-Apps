Pre-registration reward terms

What you get for pre-registering for Ocean Live, and how it works.

The reward

If you pre-register for Ocean Live on Google Play before it is released there, you get:

  • US$5 of Ocean Live credit on your Ocean Live account
  • An Early supporter badge on your account, shown in the app

How to get it

  1. Pre-register on the Ocean Live page on Google Play while pre-registration is open.
  2. Install Ocean Live from Google Play with the same Google account you pre-registered with.
  3. Open the app and sign in to your Ocean Live account. The reward is added to the account you're signed in to.

Google Play hands the reward to the app when you install it. If you sign in later, it's added the first time you open the app signed in. Claim it within 12 months after Ocean Live is released on Google Play.

Using the credit

  • You can spend it on extra "Any questions?" questions beyond the free daily ones, and on Ocean Live add-ons when they're available.
  • Credit is used before any other payment.
  • It has no cash value. It can't be exchanged for money, refunded, sold or moved to another account.
  • It doesn't expire while Ocean Live offers things to spend it on. If Ocean Live ever stops offering paid features, unused credit ends with them, and we'll say so in the app first.

Limits

  • One reward per Google account and one per Ocean Live account.
  • The badge stays on your account until you delete the account. Deleting the account also deletes any unused credit.
  • Rewards obtained by abuse (for example, many accounts made to collect credit) may be removed.
  • Pre-registration closes when Ocean Live is released on Google Play. Pre-registering doesn't oblige you to install anything, and Google can't charge you for it.

Who runs this

Ocean Live is an independent app made by an Ocean owner. Google is not a sponsor of this reward and is not responsible for it. Questions: justice@oceanlive.org.
