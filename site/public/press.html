Descriptions

Short

Battery, 12V, trips and fault codes for the Fisker Ocean, read from the car through a Bluetooth OBD adapter.

Long

Ocean Live is an independent, owner-made app for the Fisker Ocean. Through a Bluetooth OBD adapter it shows live charge, battery health measured from your own charging, a 12V battery monitor, trips and charging costs, and fault codes from 39 modules, with Android Auto, CarPlay, widgets and Wear OS and Apple Watch apps. "Any questions?" answers questions about the car and the app from an owner guide, web search and Ocean Live's GitHub. No ads, no tracking: what the app reads from your car stays on your phone.

Ocean Live is not affiliated with or endorsed by Fisker Inc.

Name: Ocean Live (two words). Platforms: Android, Wear OS, iPhone and Apple Watch. Website: oceanlive.org.
