Ocean Live news by email

New releases and important fixes, a few times a year.

Get an email when there's a new Ocean Live release or an important fix. A few emails a year, no ads. We'll send a link to confirm, and every email has an unsubscribe link. Privacy policy.

The sign-up form needs JavaScript. Without it, you can follow the releases on GitHub.

What you get

  • An email when there's a new release of the apps, with what changed.
  • An email when there's an important fix, for example one that affects your car's 12V battery or a security problem.
  • Nothing else: no ads, and your address is never shared.

How it works

  • After you sign up, we email you a link. You're only on the list once you open it and confirm. If you don't, your address is deleted after 7 days.
  • Every email has an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing deletes your address straight away.
  • Details are in the privacy policy. Prefer not to sign up? The releases on GitHub and the changelog have the same news.
