3D model

"Fisker Ocean (low-poly)" by LagzDesign, from Sketchfab, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

Changes made for this website:

  • Texture coordinates and material extensions removed; only positions and normals kept.
  • Tyres simplified: the four identical tyres share one mesh with about half the original's vertices (about 89% fewer tyre triangles in total). Meshes welded, deduplicated, quantized and compressed with meshopt.
  • On the page: drawn as hand-drawn style lines, the near-side front and rear door panels cut out of the body so they can open, the wheels turned, and the car shaded with this site's own materials and lights.
  • The static drawing (ocean-blueprint.svg) is a side view projected from the model.
  • The banner of the sign-in code email (header.png) is made from that drawing.

Neither the model's author nor Fisker Inc. made or endorses this website. No Fisker logos, wordmarks or photos are used.
