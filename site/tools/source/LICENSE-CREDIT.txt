This is the original file as downloaded from Sketchfab. It is kept here only as the source for
public/models/ocean.glb, which tools/build-model.mjs makes from it. Changes made for the website:
- texture coordinates (UVs) and material extensions removed; only positions and normals kept
- tyres simplified: the four identical tyres share one mesh with about half the original's
  vertices (about 89% fewer tyre triangles in total); meshes welded, deduplicated, quantized
  and meshopt-compressed
- on the page: drawn as lines, near-side door panels separated so they can open, and shaded
  with the site's own materials
- the static drawing (public/img/ocean-blueprint.svg) and the sign-in email banner
  (public/email/header.*) are side views projected from the model
